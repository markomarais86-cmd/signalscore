# Campaign Export Quick Start

**Export your first campaign in 5 minutes**

## Overview

This guide walks you through building and exporting your first campaign from LaunchPulse, targeting high-fit accounts with the right contacts.

**Time Required**: 5 minutes  
**Prerequisites**: Accounts scored with at least one ICP

---

## Quick Start: Export Campaign in 4 Steps

### Step 1: Navigate to Campaign Builder (30 seconds)

**Option A: From Accounts Page**
1. Navigate to **Accounts**
2. Apply filters (optional): Score Band = A
3. Click **"Build Campaign"** button

**Option B: From Campaign Page**
1. Click **"Campaigns"** in left sidebar
2. Click **"New Campaign"** button

### Step 4: Configure Deduplication & Export (1.5 minutes)

#### 4.1 Deduplication Settings

**Recommended for First Campaign:**
```
Strategy: Global Deduplication
Lookback Period: 90 days
```

This ensures contacts haven't been exported in any campaign in the last 90 days.

**Options:**

| Strategy | When to Use |
|----------|-------------|
| **Global** (Recommended) | Default for most campaigns |
| **Campaign-Specific** | Multi-touch sequences, different messaging |
| **ICP-Specific** | Separate products/ICPs |
| **None** | Testing only (not recommended) |

---

#### 4.2 Choose Export Format

**CSV Export (Recommended for First Campaign)**

Simple file download with all data:

1. Select: **"Export to CSV"**
2. Choose fields to include:
   - [x] Contact info (name, email, phone)
   - [x] Job title, seniority, department
   - [x] Company info (name, domain, website)
   - [x] Firmographics (size, industry, revenue)
   - [x] Fit score and score band
   - [x] LinkedIn URLs

3. Click **"Generate CSV"**

4. File downloads immediately:
   ```
   Q1_2024_Enterprise_SaaS_High_Fit_Campaign.csv
   ```

**Alternative Export Options:**

**Push to Salesforce:**
- Creates Campaign object
- Adds contacts as Campaign Members
- Updates Account fields with scores

**Push to HubSpot:**
- Creates static List
- Adds contacts to list
- Updates Company properties

**Push to Outreach/SalesLoft:**
- Adds contacts to sequence
- Assigns to reps
- Creates tasks

## Understanding Your Campaign

### Contact Distribution

**Healthy Mix for B2B:**
```
C-Suite: 20-30% (top decision-makers)
VP-Level: 30-40% (budget authority)
Director: 20-30% (influencers)
Manager: 10-20% (users)
```

**What It Means:**

**Top-Heavy (mostly C-Suite):**
- High-value targets
- Harder to reach
- Longer sales cycle
- Requires executive messaging

**Middle-Heavy (mostly VP/Director):**
- Balanced approach (recommended)
- Good reachability
- Decision influence
- Standard B2B outreach

**Bottom-Heavy (mostly Manager/IC):**
- Easy to reach
- Less decision authority
- May need to navigate upward
- Good for product-led growth

---

### Expected Campaign Performance

**A-Band Campaigns:**
```
Typical Metrics:
- Email Open Rate: 40-50%
- Reply Rate: 15-20%
- Positive Reply Rate: 8-12%
- Meeting Booked Rate: 5-10%
- Opportunity Created: 2-5%
```

**B-Band Campaigns:**
```
Typical Metrics:
- Email Open Rate: 35-45%
- Reply Rate: 10-15%
- Positive Reply Rate: 5-8%
- Meeting Booked Rate: 3-6%
- Opportunity Created: 1-3%
```

**Factors Affecting Performance:**
- Email copy quality
- Sender reputation
- Timing and cadence
- Offer/value proposition
- Industry/vertical

## Campaign Best Practices

### 🎯 Campaign Sizing

**First Campaign:**
- **Recommended**: 300-500 accounts
- **Why**: Manageable for testing, quick learnings
- **If smaller**: Still valuable for high-quality accounts
- **If larger**: Consider segmenting into multiple campaigns

**Subsequent Campaigns:**
- **Standard**: 500-1,000 accounts
- **Large**: 1,000-2,500 accounts
- **Mega**: 2,500+ accounts (enterprise marketing)

---

### 📅 Campaign Timing

**When to Launch:**

**Best Days:**
- Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday (highest engagement)

**Best Times:**
- 8-10 AM local time (inbox top-of-mind)
- 12-2 PM local time (lunch break)
- 4-6 PM local time (end of day check)

**Avoid:**
- Monday (busy catching up)
- Friday (mentally checked out)
- Weekends (low B2B email checking)
- Major holidays

---

### ✉️ Multi-Touch Sequences

Don't send one email and give up. Use sequences:

**Recommended Sequence:**
```
Day 0: Initial email (personalized)
Day 3: Follow-up email (value-add)
Day 7: Final email (breakup)
Day 14: LinkedIn connection request
Day 21: Phone call (if high-value)
```

**LaunchPulse Integration:**
- Export includes all contact data
- Use in your email tool (Outreach, SalesLoft, Mailchimp)
- Track results in CRM
- Re-import outcomes to LaunchPulse

---

### 📊 Track Campaign Results

**Key Metrics to Monitor:**

1. **Delivery Metrics**
   - Bounce rate (<5% is healthy)
   - Unsubscribe rate (<0.5% is healthy)
   - Spam complaint rate (<0.1% critical)

2. **Engagement Metrics**
   - Open rate (>35% is good)
   - Reply rate (>10% is good)
   - Click rate (if applicable)

3. **Conversion Metrics**
   - Meeting booked (track in CRM)
   - Opportunity created
   - Revenue generated

**Feed Results Back to LaunchPulse:**
- Tag accounts that converted
- Upload closed-won deals
- Refine ICP based on learnings
- Improve feature weights

## Troubleshooting

### ❌ Issue: "No contacts found"

**Causes:**
- Persona filters too restrictive
- Accounts lack contact data
- All contacts previously exported

**Solutions:**
1. Broaden job title criteria
2. Add more seniority levels
3. Run contact discovery/enrichment
4. Reduce deduplication lookback period
5. Check CRM sync for contact import

---

### ❌ Issue: "Very few contacts per account"

**Causes:**
- Narrow persona filters
- Contact data incomplete
- Low data quality

**Solutions:**
1. Expand persona criteria (add more titles)
2. Increase max contacts per account (2 → 3)
3. Run contact enrichment (Apollo, ZoomInfo)
4. Enable contact discovery automation
5. Import contacts from CRM

---

### ❌ Issue: "Export failed"

**Causes:**
- CRM authentication expired
- Rate limit exceeded
- Network timeout
- Invalid field mapping

**Solutions:**
1. Re-authenticate CRM connection
2. Wait 15 minutes and retry (rate limit)
3. Try CSV export instead of CRM push
4. Check error logs in Settings → Export History
5. Contact support with error details

---

### ❌ Issue: "Wrong contacts exported"

**Causes:**
- Persona filters misconfigured
- Prioritization not applied
- Data quality issues

**Solutions:**
1. Review persona filter settings
2. Check prioritization method
3. Preview contacts before export
4. Run data quality check
5. Re-export with corrected filters
