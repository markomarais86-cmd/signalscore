# Score Bands (A/B/C/D)

## Overview

Score bands group fit scores into discrete categories for easy segmentation and campaign targeting.

## Default Band Definitions

| Band | Score Range | Label | Use Case |
|------|-------------|-------|----------|
| **A** | 80-100 | High Fit | Direct sales outreach |
| **B** | 60-79 | Medium-High Fit | Sales campaigns |
| **C** | 40-59 | Medium Fit | Marketing nurture |
| **D** | 0-39 | Low Fit | Exclude from campaigns |

## Distribution Analysis

### Healthy Distribution
- A: 10-20% (top tier)
- B: 25-35% (qualified)
- C: 30-40% (nurture)
- D: 15-25% (exclude)

## Customizing Bands

Bands can be customized in Settings → Scoring → Score Bands.
